# Heron's formula

Statement: A triangle with sides a, b and c and semiperimeter s = (a + b + c)/2 has the area √(s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)).
Intuition: Three side lengths already fix a triangle completely, and so they fix its area. Heron's formula computes the area straight from the sides, with no height and no angle.
Examples: For the sides 3, 4 and 5, s = 6 and the area is √(6 · 3 · 2 · 1) = √36 = 6. For the sides 13, 14 and 15, s = 21 and the area is √(21 · 8 · 7 · 6) = √7056 = 84.
Misconception: s is half the perimeter, not the whole perimeter. For the sides 3, 4 and 5, s = 6, not 12.
