# Multiplication

Statement: Multiplying adds the same number again and again. 3 · 4 = 12: three groups of four things are twelve things, 4 + 4 + 4 = 12. The numbers you multiply are the factors, and the result is the product.
Intuition: Lay the things out in rows: 3 rows of 4 make a rectangle with 12 things. Turn it and you see 4 rows of 3, so 4 · 3 is also 12.
Examples: 2 · 5 = 10. 6 · 7 = 42. Anything times 0 is 0, and anything times 1 stays what it is.
Misconception: Multiplying does not always make a number bigger. 5 · 1 is still 5, and 5 · 0 is 0.
