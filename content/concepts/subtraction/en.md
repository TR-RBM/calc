# Subtraction

Statement: Subtracting takes an amount away. 7 − 3 = 4: from seven things you take three away, and four are left. The result is the difference.
Intuition: Subtraction undoes addition. 7 − 3 asks: what do you add to 3 to get 7? Count up from 3 to 7, that is four steps.
Examples: 5 − 2 = 3. 42 − 17 = 25, because 17 + 25 = 42.
Misconception: With subtraction the order matters. 7 − 3 is 4, but 3 − 7 is not 4: taking seven away from three things needs numbers below zero.
