# Law of sines

Statement: In any triangle the ratio of a side to the sine of its opposite angle is the same for all three sides, a / sin α = b / sin β = c / sin γ.
Intuition: The longest side of a triangle lies opposite the largest angle. The law of sines makes this exact: if you divide a side by the sine of the angle opposite it, you get the same number for all three sides.
Examples: With α = 30°, β = 45° and a = 4, b = a · sin β / sin α = 4 · (√2/2) / (1/2) = 4√2.
Misconception: Knowing sin β does not always fix the angle β. If sin β = ½, β can be 30° or 150°, and you have to check which angle fits the triangle.
