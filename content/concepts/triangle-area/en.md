# Area of a triangle

Statement: The area of a triangle is one half of the base times the height, A = ½bh. With two sides and the included angle it is A = ½ab·sin γ.
Intuition: Two copies of the same triangle fit together into a four-sided shape with the same base and the same height. The triangle is half of it, which is why ½ stands in front of bh.
Examples: A triangle with base 6 cm and height 4 cm has area ½ · 6 cm · 4 cm = 12 cm². A triangle with sides 8 cm and 5 cm and the angle 30° between them has area ½ · 8 cm · 5 cm · sin 30° = 10 cm², because sin 30° = ½.
Misconception: The height is not just a slanted side. It stands at a right angle on the base and reaches the opposite corner. Only in a right triangle is one of the sides also the height.
