# Triangle

Statement: A triangle has three sides and three angles, and the measures of its angles add up to 180°.
Intuition: Cut a triangle out of paper, tear off its three corners and put their points together. Side by side they make a straight line, and that is 180°.
Examples: If a triangle has angles of 90° and 30°, its third angle is 180° − 90° − 30° = 60°. A triangle with three equal angles has 60° at each corner.
Misconception: Longer sides do not make the angles bigger. A big and a small triangle of the same shape have the same angles, and in every triangle they add up to 180°.
