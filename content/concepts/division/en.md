# Division

Statement: Dividing shares an amount into equal parts. 12 ÷ 4 = 3: twelve things shared fairly among four give each three. The result is the quotient.
Intuition: Division undoes multiplication. 12 ÷ 4 asks: how many times does 4 fit into 12? Because 3 · 4 = 12, the answer is 3.
Examples: 10 ÷ 2 = 5. 42 ÷ 6 = 7, because 6 · 7 = 42. 7 ÷ 2 is 3 with 1 left over, or 7/2 as a fraction.
Misconception: You cannot divide by 0. Sharing twelve things among no one gives no answer, because no number times 0 is 12.
