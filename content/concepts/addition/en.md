# Addition

Statement: Adding puts two amounts together. 3 + 4 = 7: three things and four more things are seven things. The numbers you add are the addends, and the result is the sum.
Intuition: Count on from the first number: from 3, count four steps further, 4, 5, 6, 7, and you land on 7. The order does not matter, 4 + 3 is also 7.
Examples: 1 + 1 = 2. 25 + 17 = 42, because 20 + 10 = 30 and 5 + 7 = 12, and 30 + 12 = 42.
Misconception: When you add numbers with several digits, ones go with ones and tens with tens. 25 + 17 is not 2 + 1 and 5 + 7 written next to each other (312); the 12 ones make one more ten.
