# Diameter of a circle

Statement: The diameter of a circle is twice its radius, d = 2r.
Intuition: The diameter goes from the circle through the centre to the other side. It is made of two radii lying end to end.
Examples: If a circle has radius 5 cm, its diameter is 2 · 5 cm = 10 cm. If a circle has diameter 8 m, its radius is 8 m ÷ 2 = 4 m.
Misconception: Not every segment across the circle is a diameter. Only the one through the centre is, and every other segment from the circle to the circle is shorter.
