# Circle

Statement: A circle is described by the length r of its radius. The length of its diameter is d = 2r, and its circumference is its perimeter.
Intuition: Every point on the circle is the same distance from the centre. If you tie a string to a pencil, hold the other end still and draw the pencil around, it draws a circle, and the string is its radius.
Examples: A circle with radius 4 cm has diameter 8 cm. A circle with radius 1 m has diameter 2 m.
Misconception: The radius and the diameter are not the same. The radius goes from the centre to the circle, the diameter goes all the way across through the centre and is twice as long.
